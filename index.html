SLM · Employee self-service

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the SLM App handles information when employees use its work and account features.

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Who this policy covers

The SLM App is an employee self-service application used with an employer-managed account. The SSBI Group entity that employs you (your “Employer”) administers your employment records and determines how those records are used. The app connects to SLM services operated for the Employer. This policy applies to the app and its related services.

Information the app handles

Depending on the features enabled by your Employer, the app may handle:

  • Account and profile information: work email, employee identifier, name, phone number, job or department details, reporting relationships, and assigned work location.
  • Employment records: attendance and punch-in/punch-out times, leave requests and balances, work-from-home requests and reports, policy acknowledgements, and related approvals.
  • Work activity: official-visit plans and status, meeting notes, leads, reports, destinations, and information entered about business contacts or participants.
  • Authentication and technical information: sign-in credentials submitted for authentication, session tokens stored using the device’s secure storage, and technical request details used to operate and troubleshoot the service.
  • Location-related information: work locations or destinations you enter, and location fields associated with attendance or visit records when provided by the service. The app does not request device location permission in its current Android configuration.

How information is used

The Employer and SLM services use this information to authenticate employees, provide employee self-service features, maintain attendance and employment records, route requests for review or approval, support work visits and reporting, administer company policies, and protect and troubleshoot the service.

How information is shared

Information is made available to the Employer and its authorized personnel, such as HR staff, managers, or administrators, as needed for the features and employment processes described above. It may also be processed by service providers that host, maintain, secure, or support the service on the Employer’s behalf. Information may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the rights and security of users and the service.

The app is designed for workplace administration and does not use employee information for third-party advertising.

Storage, security, and retention

App requests are sent to the SLM service configured for your organization. Production service connections use HTTPS, and authentication tokens are stored using platform secure storage. The Employer and service operator use administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information; no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Employment and activity records are retained according to the Employer’s retention schedule and applicable legal requirements. Contact your HR or privacy contact for information about the retention period that applies to your records.

Your choices and requests

Your Employer manages employee accounts. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account or associated information, contact your HR team or the privacy contact below. The Employer may need to retain certain records for employment, audit, or legal purposes and will handle requests under applicable requirements.

Children’s privacy

The SLM App is intended for employees and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact your HR team or email info@ssbi.in with “SLM Privacy” in the subject line.
